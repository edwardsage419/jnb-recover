# Code of Conduct

## English

JNB Recover welcomes technical and scientific collaboration conducted with professional respect.

Contributors should discuss evidence, code, compatibility, and scientific interpretation on their merits. Disagreement about parser behavior or scientific semantics should be resolved through reproducible fixtures, independent references, tests, and clearly stated uncertainty.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory abuse, deliberate disclosure of private information, and knowingly unsafe publication of confidential or restricted scientific files are not acceptable.

Project discussions should stay relevant to read-only JNB recovery, validation, provenance, interoperability, packaging, documentation, and maintainable offline workflows.

Maintainers may edit, hide, close, or remove contributions that violate these expectations or expose sensitive material.

---

## 简体中文

JNB Recover 欢迎以专业和相互尊重为基础的技术与科研协作。

讨论应围绕证据、代码、兼容性和科学解释本身展开。对于解析器行为或科学语义的分歧，应通过可复现 fixture、独立参考、测试以及明确表达不确定性来解决。

骚扰、人身攻击、歧视性攻击、故意公开私人信息，以及明知存在风险仍发布机密或受限制科研文件的行为不可接受。

项目讨论应保持在只读 JNB 恢复、验证、provenance、互操作、打包、文档和可维护离线工作流的范围内。

维护者可以编辑、隐藏、关闭或移除违反这些要求，或暴露敏感材料的贡献内容。
